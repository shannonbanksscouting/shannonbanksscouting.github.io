Cú Chulainn Cub Pack News

Updates for Thursday Cubs — Shane, Evelyn, Martin & Eibhlín

7 May 2026 Notice

No Cub Meeting — Thursday 14th May

There will be no cub meeting on Thursday 14th May, as the Scouts will be using the hall to organise their gear for the Annual Scout County Shield.

Normal meetings resume the following Thursday.

7 May 2026 All Sections

Group Camp — Killaloe, 5th–7th June

Our next big event is the Group Camp in Killaloe Scout Campsite (Clarisford) on the weekend of 5th to 7th June 2026.

This is a fun camp with all sections together (Beavers, Cubs, Scouts, Venturers and Rovers) to celebrate 50 years of Scouting in Shannon Banks. It will be similar to the group camp held last year in Shannon.

Cubs are welcome to come and go as they wish if they have other commitments. More information including cost and programme will follow — please save the date!

7 May 2026 Competition

National Cub Challenge — Dublin

Congratulations to both teams who performed fantastically at the County Cub Challenge! The "Icebreaker Six" achieved Gold Standard and First Place and have been selected to represent Shannon Banks at the National Cub Challenge.

When: 20th & 21st June 2026 (travelling Friday 19th)
Where: Larch Hill Scout Campsite, Dublin

The team will camp overnight on Friday in advance of the competition start. A couple more training sessions will take place between now and then. We will rent a van for gear transport but will need parent volunteers to ferry cubs up and back.

7 May 2026 Info

Moving Up to Scouts

Seven cubs born in 2014 will finish their Cub journey at the end of June 2026 and move on to Scouts in September 2026.

A further three cubs (born January–April 2015) will be overage for the 2027 Cub Challenge Competition. These cubs have the option of staying with Cubs for another year but will be unable to compete as part of a "six" at either the County or National Cub Challenge 2027.

Parents of these cubs should speak to Shane if they'd like advice on whether to stay or move up.

7 May 2026 Important

Scouter Changes from September 2026

Some changes to our leadership team from next season:

  • Evelyn will "retire" from Cubs after six incredible years — she's transferring to the Scout section. A huge thank you for her contribution!
  • Shane will finish as Section Leader after three years, taking over the Scout Troop from Mike Ryan.
  • Caitlín Ní Chaoindealbháin will take over as Section Leader for the Cú Chulainn Cub Pack. Caitlín is well known to the cubs and has supported the last two County Cub Challenges in a lead role.
  • Eibhlín Ní Phriosáin and Martin Smith will remain to ensure continuity.

We wish Caitlín all the best and thank Shane and Evelyn for everything!

7 May 2026 Date

End of Year — Final Meeting

Our final cub meeting of the 2025/2026 year will be on Thursday 25th June 2026.
