Cub Scouts

Building skills, confidence and friendships through badge work, camping and teamwork

What are Cubs?

Cub Scouts is for young people aged 9 to 11. Cubs build on the fun of Beavers with more challenging activities, badge work and their first real camping experiences.

We run two Cub packs — Wednesday and Thursday evenings.

What We Do

  • Camping and outdoor cooking
  • Badge work across many skills
  • Team challenges and problem solving
  • Hiking and nature exploration
  • Pioneering and knot craft
  • Community service projects
