Scout Troop News

Updates for Friday Scouts — Mike Ryan & team

May 2026 Achievement

Gold Standard at the Scout County Shield!

Congratulations to all our Scouts on working so hard over the weekend to achieve the Gold Standard in Campcraft at the County Shield in Pallaskenry!

Shannon Banks sent three patrols of 8 scouts — a huge turnout — and we the Leaders are very proud of their achievements.

May 2026 Notice

No Scout Meeting This Friday

There will be no Scout meeting this Friday as the Beavers are having an overnight camp in the field.

Normal meetings will resume the following Friday.

May 2026 Help Needed

Help Needed — Wednesday 7:30pm

We would appreciate some help on Wednesday night at 7:30pm to unload the trailer and fold the tents from the Shield.

Many hands make light work — all parents welcome!

May 2026 Lost Property

Lost Scout Shirt — Pallaskenry

A Shannon Banks Scout left their Scout shirt behind in Pallaskenry after the Shield. A leader has it — please check with your Scout and let us know if it's yours.
