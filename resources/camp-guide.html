Beaver Overnight Camp Guide

Everything parents need to know about Beaver sleepovers at the Scout Hall

Event Overview

The Beaver overnight is a highlight of the year — their first big sleepover adventure! Beavers camp in or next to the Scout Hall (not in tents in the open field like older sections).

Location

Shannon Banks Scout Hall & grounds, Corbally, Limerick (V94 K72O). Beavers sleep inside the hall or in tents pitched right beside it.

Weather Policy

Rain or shine — we go ahead! Beavers always have the hall as a backup, so bad weather is never a problem.

Cost

No extra charge — covered under existing membership fees.

Sample Schedule

A typical Beaver overnight timeline. Times are approximate.

Friday Evening

7:00 PM
Arrival & Drop-off

Parents drop off at the Scout Hall. Quick hello, settle in, and get excited!

7:15 PM
Set Up Sleeping Area

Lay out sleeping bags and mats inside the hall or set up tents right beside it.

8:00 PM
Cooked Dinner

A hot meal cooked outdoors.

8:45 PM
Campfire & Songs

Gather round the campfire for songs, stories, and craic.

9:30 PM
S'mores & Marshmallows

The highlight of the night! Gooey, melty, delicious.

Bedtime

10:30 PM
Pyjamas & Wind Down

Time to get cozy. Pyjamas on, teeth brushed, settle into tents.

11:00 PM
Lights Out

Into the tents, teddies at the ready. Goodnight!

Saturday Morning

6:00–7:00 AM
Wake Up & Breakfast

They'll be up early and buzzing! A good breakfast to start.

7:30 AM
Nature Walk

A gentle walk in the fresh morning air.

8:30 AM
Tidy Up & Tent Take-Down

Leave no trace! Pack everything away.

10:00 AM
Collection by Parents

Pick up your (probably tired but very happy!) Beaver.

What to Bring

Let them help pack their own bag — it's part of the adventure!

🛏️ Sleeping Bag
🛋️ Pillow
🧻 Roll Mat
🧸 Teddy / Comfort Toy
🪥 Toothbrush & Paste
👕 Pyjamas
👟 Change of Clothes
🧥 Warm Jacket
🌧️ Rain Gear
🔦 Torch (Labelled)
💧 Water Bottle
🏷️ All Items Labelled!
