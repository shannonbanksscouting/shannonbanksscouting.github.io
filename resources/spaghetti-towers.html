STEM Activity

Spaghetti & Marshmallow Towers

A fun STEM activity to learn about engineering and teamwork

What You'll Need

🍝

Dry Spaghetti

Uncooked spaghetti strands — the longer the better!

🍬

Mini Marshmallows

These act as the "joints" to connect your spaghetti.

🧠

Creativity!

Your imagination is your most important tool.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Preparation

Give each small group a handful of dry spaghetti and a bowl of mini marshmallows.

Tip: Work in groups of 2–3 for best teamwork!
2

The Base

Start by creating a flat geometric shape (square or triangle) on the table. Use marshmallows as the "joints" to connect the spaghetti strands.

3

Building Up (Towers)

  • Push vertical spaghetti strands into the corner marshmallows
  • Connect the tops with more marshmallows and horizontal strands
  • Create a cube or pyramid shape
Super Tip: Use triangles in your design — they are much stronger and more stable than squares!
4

Spanning Gaps (Bridges)

  • Build two sturdy towers first
  • Connect them using long horizontal strands
  • Reinforce the "roadway" by bundling 2–3 strands together
Tip: Use tape or more marshmallows to bundle strands together — this prevents snapping!

What We're Learning

📐

Triangles are Strong!

A triangle keeps its shape under pressure, while a square can collapse. That's why bridges use triangular shapes!

⚖️

Balance & Stability

A wide base helps keep structures from falling over. The wider the base, the more stable the tower!

💪

Tension & Compression

Some parts are being pushed (compression) and others pulled (tension). Engineers think about both!

🤝

Teamwork

The best structures are built by teams working together, just like real engineers!
