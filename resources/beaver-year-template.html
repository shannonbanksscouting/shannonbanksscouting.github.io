📝 Scout Law Game

Objective: Reinforce the new lodges and introduce the Beaver Scout Law in a fun, competitive way.

  • Preparation: Write the words of the Beaver Scout Law on separate slips of paper — one set per lodge.
  • The Challenge: Each lodge gets an envelope with jumbled words and must arrange them correctly: “A Beaver Scout has fun, and is kind and helpful.”
  • The Race: First team to correctly lay out the law wins.
  • Fall In: Everyone recites the Beaver Scout Law together.

🧭 Compass Making Instructions

Materials needed: A leaf, a paper clip, a strong magnet, a small bowl of water

  1. Magnetize the paper clip: Rub it repeatedly in one direction along the magnet (20–30 times).
  2. Float the leaf: Gently place the leaf flat on the surface of the water.
  3. Place the paper clip: Carefully lay the magnetized paper clip on top of the floating leaf.
  4. Watch it turn: The paper clip will slowly turn until one end points north!

📣 Beep Beep Game

  • Setup: Beavers get into pairs and decide on a secret signal only they know
  • Play: One partner is blindfolded and spun around
  • Challenge: The seeing partner calls out their secret signal
  • Goal: The blindfolded partner must find their partner using only sound

🔥 Tea Bag Rocket Experiment

Materials: Funnel tea bags, square silver tin foil pieces, lighter (adult use only)

  1. Snip both ends of tea bags and remove all tea leaves
  2. Place the empty tea bag upright on tin foil
  3. Light the top — as it burns down, the ash floats to the ceiling!
  4. Each Beaver gets their own turn

🤔 Two Truths and One Lie

Beavers sit in a circle. Each shares 2 truths and 1 lie about themselves — the group votes on which is the lie.

🧭 Compass Making

Materials (bring if you can): Magnet, paper clip, cork, small bowl of water (provided).

  1. Magnetize the paper clip (rub along magnet 20–30 times)
  2. Cut a thin slice of cork so it floats flat
  3. Float the cork on water
  4. Place magnetized paper clip on cork
  5. Watch it point north!

Planning Tips

Things to keep in mind when building your Beaver year

🌦️

Plan A / Plan B

Always have an indoor backup. Weather is unpredictable — have a games night or craft ready to go.

🔄

Vary the Mix

Alternate between outdoor, craft, games, visits, and skills. Don't do two crafts in a row!

📅

Book Early

Visits and guest speakers need lead time. Confirm special events at least 4 weeks ahead.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Involvement

Invite parents to special events — investitures, carol singing, and the end-of-year party.

🏅

Badge Integration

Tie activities to Scouting Ireland badge requirements where possible — double the value!

⏰

Keep It Short

Beavers are 6–8. Sessions are 1.5 hours. Plan 2–3 short activities rather than one long one.
