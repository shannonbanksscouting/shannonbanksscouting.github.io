A Typical Beaver Year

Sample week-by-week programme from September to June — adapt it for your section!

This is a real-world example of a full Beaver Scout year. It shows the mix of crafts, outdoor activities, visits, games, and special events that make up a balanced programme. Use it as inspiration when planning your own section's year.

Planning Tips

Things to keep in mind when building your Beaver year

🌦️

Plan A / Plan B

Always have an indoor backup. Weather is unpredictable — have a games night or craft ready to go.

🔄

Vary the Mix

Alternate between outdoor, craft, games, visits, and skills. Don't do two crafts in a row!

📅

Book Early

Visits and guest speakers need lead time. Confirm special events at least 4 weeks ahead.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Involvement

Invite parents to special events — investitures, carol singing, and the end-of-year party.

🏅

Badge Integration

Tie activities to Scouting Ireland badge requirements where possible — double the value!

⏰

Keep It Short

Beavers are 6–8. Sessions are 1 hour. Plan 2–3 short activities rather than one long one.
