Beaver Scouts

Fun, friendship and adventure — the start of every young person's scouting journey

What are Beavers?

Beaver Scouts is the youngest section in Scouting Ireland, for children aged 6 to 8. Through games, stories, crafts and outdoor activities, Beavers develop confidence, make friends and learn new skills in a safe, fun environment.

Our group runs two Beaver colonies — Monday and Tuesday evenings — giving families more flexibility.

What We Do

  • Nature walks and outdoor exploration
  • Arts, crafts and creative projects
  • Games and teamwork challenges
  • Badge work and skill building
  • Day trips and sleepovers
  • Community and environmental projects
